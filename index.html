01 / About

Research grounded in requirements.

My research examines how natural language processing and machine learning can support the analysis of security requirements. I focus on connecting software specifications with security standards and identifying gaps in IoT smart home requirements.

I completed my PhD in Information Technology at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, Malaysia. My doctoral work investigated a BERT-based framework for assessing the completeness of smart home security requirements using Common Criteria.

Alongside research, I teach computing and software engineering, drawing on experience in higher education and software support.

02 / Text classificationOngoing research

Lightweight security requirements classification

Exploring classification of requirements into Common Criteria functional classes.

Approach
Frozen MiniLM embeddings, a gated residual classifier and a TF-IDF logistic regression branch combined through probability fusion.
Evaluation scope
CPN, ePurse and GlobalPlatform requirements, focused on FCO, FIA and FTP.
