01 / Profile

Bridging AI, Standards, and Cyber Resilience.

My work centers on Security Requirements Engineering (SRE), Machine Learning / NLP, and IoT ecosystems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Information Technology at Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP), investigating BERT-NLP frameworks to audit and assess the completeness of smart home IoT security requirements using international security criteria (Common Criteria / ISO 15408).

Currently, as a Lecturer at the British International College of Krirk University (Thailand), I combine student-centered technical instruction with collaborative research across Malaysia, Pakistan, and Southeast Asia.
