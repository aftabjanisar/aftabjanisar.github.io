02 / Professional profile

Professional summary.

An academic and researcher specializing in AI-enabled security requirements engineering, software engineering and IoT security. Holds a PhD in Information Technology from Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS, with doctoral research on BERT-based assessment of smart home security requirements against Common Criteria.

Published in international journals and conferences, including the IEEE Internet of Things Journal, Automated Software Engineering and Scientific African. Recipient of the Best Research Paper Award for the 2023 IEEE ICSECS paper on security requirements assurance. Contributed to a cross-institutional research grant by writing the proposal and completing the project.

Teaching experience spans institutions in Thailand, Malaysia and Pakistan, covering software and requirements engineering, information systems, programming and computing ethics. Combines research, student project supervision and earlier system and software support experience, with an emphasis on practical learning and collaborative problem solving.
